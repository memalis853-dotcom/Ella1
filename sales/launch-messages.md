## Tips

- **Photos sell.** Post the lunch box, cake and drinks photos — real food from your kitchen works better than designs.
- **Post your testimonials.** Screenshot the 4 reviews (hide surnames/numbers) and post one each day.
- **Reply to every comment and DM** quickly — that's where most sales happen.
- **Ask your first buyers** for a short message or photo of a meal they made. Each one is a new testimonial.
- **Avoid "cure" or "heal autism" claims** in posts and ads. Facebook can reject them, and it protects you. Say "support", "helped my family", "one step at a time".
