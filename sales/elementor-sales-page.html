Here's what nobody told you

It was never just about “picky eating”.

1

Allergens hide in “safe” foods

Pap, custard, Milo, stock cubes, baking powder, soy sauce, many “gluten-free” products — gluten, corn, dairy and sugar hide in foods we eat every day. You can't avoid what you can't see.

2

A calmer gut can mean a calmer child

Many parents notice that when the gut is irritated, everything feels harder — sleep, moods, toilet troubles and picky eating. Removing the irritants and adding healing foods is where many families start.

3

Picky eating is often about feeling safe

A child who eats only a few foods is often a child whose senses feel overwhelmed. Pressure makes it worse. Gentle steps, familiar foods and fun shapes make it better.

Put those together and you get exactly what you're living through. Not a parenting problem — a plan problem. And that's exactly what the Healing Collection gives you.
