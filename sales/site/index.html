Frequently asked questions

How do I get the books?

Straight after payment you'll receive a download link for all 6 books. Read them on your phone, tablet or computer — or print them.

What format are the books in?

Beautifully designed PDFs, plus Word versions so you can make notes. There's also one all-in-one PDF with all 6 books together.

I don't live in Nigeria — will I find the ingredients?

Yes. Recipes include simple swaps, and there's a brands guide for Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, South Africa, the UK, the USA and Canada. Half of the meals are international family favourites.

My child is a very picky eater. Will this work for us?

This collection was made with picky eaters in mind — hidden vegetables, blended soups, dips, fun shapes and a gentle picky eater playbook. Go slowly, one new food at a time, next to a food your child already loves.

Are the recipes nut-free?

All lunch box and party recipes are nut-free and school-safe. The family meals use seeds like pumpkin and sunflower instead of nuts.

Is this a cure for autism?

No. Food is not a cure, and this collection is not medical advice. It shares what helped one family. Many parents find a calmer gut helps with sleep, mood and picky eating. Please keep working with your child's doctor, and check with them before giving herbs or supplements.

Can I share the books?

The books are for your family's personal use. If a friend would love them, please share this page — it helps me keep creating.
