**How do I get the books?**
Straight after payment, you'll get a download link for all 6 books. You can read them on your phone, tablet or computer, or print them.

**What format are they in?**
PDF (beautifully designed) **and** Word, so you can print or make notes. There's also an all-in-one PDF with all 6 books together.

**I don't live in Nigeria — will I find the ingredients?**
Yes. There are swaps on the recipes and a brands guide for Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, South Africa, the UK, the USA and Canada. Half of the meals are international family favourites.

**Are the recipes nut-free?**
The lunch box and party recipes are completely nut-free. The family meals use seeds (like pumpkin and sunflower) instead of nuts.

**Is this a cure for autism?**
No. Food is not a cure, and this collection is not medical advice. It shares what helped one family. Many parents find that a calmer gut helps with sleep, mood and picky eating. Always work with your child's doctor, and check with them before giving herbs or supplements.

**Can I share the books with friends?**
The books are for your family's personal use. If a friend would love them, please send them the link — it helps me keep creating.
