## Food to Eat (page 3)

1. **Root vegetable carbs** — best source of carbohydrates: squash, sweet potato, pumpkin, yams. They are packed with phytonutrients, offer protected sugar and help to chelate heavy metals out of your system.
2. **Green leafy vegetables** — anti-inflammatory compounds and help open up elimination pathways. Broccoli sprouts should be top of the list.
3. **Wild blueberries and mangoes** — all fruits are great, but these are particularly beneficial for cleansing the brain and supporting the adrenal glands.
4. **Coriander and parsley** — these culinary herbs are great options to reach deep into the brain to pull toxic metals out of the brain.
